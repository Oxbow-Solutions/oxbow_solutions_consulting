Services

From field images to defensible results

Computer vision phenotyping

Custom detection, segmentation and counting models for seeds, roots, canopy cover and plots. Semi-supervised training gets you there with fewer hand-labeled images.

Statistics & experimental design

Mixed-effects models, meta-analysis and trial design in R or Python, so field and greenhouse experiments answer the question you actually asked.

Research data infrastructure

Databases, crop ontologies and FAIR data practices. Your lab's data stays findable and reusable, and it's ready to publish on Zenodo or Hugging Face.

Deployment & web tools

Serverless GPU inference, REST APIs and web apps your technicians and collaborators can use from a browser without installing anything.

How it works

A simple, collaborative process

  1. Scope

    A free call to understand your research question, data and constraints.

  2. Prototype

    A quick proof of concept on your own images or data, so you can see value before committing.

  3. Build

    Production-ready models, analyses or tools, built in close collaboration with your team.

  4. Hand off

    Documented, reproducible code and training, so your lab owns the result.

About

A plant scientist who builds models

I'm Bo Meyering, founder of Oxbow Solutions. I spent more than a decade in plant science, at USDA-ARS, the University of Florida, and most recently The Land Institute, where I led deep learning phenotyping for the perennial legumes program.

Today I'm also Director of Computer Vision at InFlightAI, where I lead a team taking computer vision models from research to production. Oxbow brings that production experience back to agricultural research labs.

I speak both languages: experimental design and field trials as well as PyTorch and cloud deployment. That means less translation and results your reviewers will trust.
